Privacy Policy — Splitloop

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Splitloop is a puzzle game published by greybits. This policy explains what the app stores, what it shares, and who to contact about it. It is written to be accurate rather than reassuring: where data does leave your device, it says so.

Contact: greysonmiller67@gmail.com

The short version

  • Splitloop has no account, no login and no sign-up.
  • Your progress, coins, cosmetics and settings never leave your device.
  • The app shows ads, and the ad provider collects data to serve them.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by the app store. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not build a profile of you.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the game is stored locally, in the app's own storage:

  • level progress, stars and which packs are unlocked
  • your best Endless score, and the score after each cut of that best run, which the game uses to show how a new run compares
  • your Daily streak and daily-mission progress
  • your Blobdex: the blob values you have made in each colour
  • coins, owned and equipped cosmetics, and booster quantities
  • your settings — sound, music, volume, blob speed, special blobs, vibration, theme, reduce motion, colour-blind shapes
  • how many rewarded ads you have watched toward a cosmetic

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no server holding your game. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, and there is no way for us to recover it afterwards.

Advertising

The free version of Splitloop shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. There are four kinds: a banner at the end of a screen, an ad between games, an ad when opening the app, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch, either to unlock a skin, bowl or cut trail, or to continue a game that has just ended.

To serve those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate, country-level location derived from it
  • device and app information — model, operating system version, app version, language
  • how you interact with an ad — that it was shown, and whether you tapped it

Google acts as an independent controller of that data. What it does with it is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can remove all advertising with a one-time in-app purchase. Once ads are removed, the advertising SDK is not initialised at all and none of the above is collected.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Before any ad is requested, Splitloop shows you Google's consent form and asks whether ads may use your data. Nothing is requested until you have answered. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices, which reopens the same form.

Purchases

Splitloop offers optional one-time purchases: removing ads, two cosmetic items, a booster bundle, and a tip jar that unlocks nothing.

Purchases are processed entirely by the app store you installed the game from. The store tells the app which items you own so it can unlock them. We never see or receive your name, card number, billing address or any other payment detail — the app only ever learns "this item is owned".

Your purchases are tied to your store account, so reinstalling the game restores them.

Analytics

Splitloop contains no analytics SDK. We do not track sessions, screens, funnels or events, and we receive no reports about how you play.

Children

Splitloop is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact greysonmiller67@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Data we hold about you

None. We operate no servers and have no database. Because the game has no account, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete — deleting the app removes your data completely.

Requests concerning data held by Google (advertising) or by the app store (purchases) must be made to those companies, using the links above and your store account settings, because we have no access to it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data about you, and to object to it being used for advertising. Since we hold no personal data ourselves, those rights are exercised:

  • For advertising — through the consent form in the app (Settings → Ad privacy choices) and

through your device's advertising settings, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.

  • For purchases — through your app store account.
  • For everything stored in the game — by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top changes with it, and the updated policy appears both here and inside the app under Settings → Privacy Policy.
